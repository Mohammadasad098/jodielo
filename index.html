Jodie Lo is a songwriter and artist creating her own corner of soul pop. A global songwriter at Musikmakarna, she turns the messy, tender, occasionally confusing bits of being human into songs that feel like they get you.

Come for a melody. Stay for the words. Put on your favourite track and make a little room for yourself.
